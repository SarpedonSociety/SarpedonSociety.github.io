Welcome to the Sarpedon Society's home on the internet. 

A place dedicated to recording the stories and lives of the men who served in the Navy during World War II. My current focus is on the Pacific submarine fleet but my research covers a range of ships and roles for men drawn from other areas. Looking forward to the next 190 stories.
Check out the [[Silversides]] [[Prosopography]] for the initial research that I have completed with the crew.
