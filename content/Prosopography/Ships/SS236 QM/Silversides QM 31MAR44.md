| Name | SN | Quarter of Change | From | To |
|------|----|------------------|------|-----|
| MARSH | [[393-36-39]] | Dec 42 | F1c | MoMM2c |
| MC MULLEN | [[258-27-84]] | Dec 42 | F1c | MoMM2c |

| RUHL | [[614-27-98]] | Mar 44 | F1c | MoMM2c |
| SHOFF | [[600-24-62]] | Mar 44 | F1c | MoMM2c |

This is a systematic rerating — experienced Firemen being qualified and redesignated as Motor Machinist's Mates. The submarine's engineering department was converting its fire room crew into engine room specialists.
