## E/R Ambiguity Resolution

This muster resolves several E/R misreads from the September 1942 transcription:

| Name | SN | Sept 42 (misread) | Dec 42 (confirmed) | Corrected Reading |
|------|-----|-------------------|-------------------|------------------|
| CALVER | [[238-66-06]] | RM2c | EM2c→EM1c | Was always EM |
| CLARK, Arthur | [[381-23-32]] | RM1c | EM1c | Was always EM |
| HART | [[269-02-95]] | RM3c | EM3c | Was always EM |
| KELLY | [[201-52-69]] | ⚠️EM1c/MM1c | EM1c | Confirmed EM1c |

**STEGALL** ([[295-28-92]]) shows "CRM(AA)" on this muster — the same letter ambiguity persists. Given the pattern above, this is likely "CEM(AA)" (Chief Electrician's Mate), making his full progression EM1c → CEM(AA) → CEM(A).

**HAGER** ([[382-51-13]]) still reads as "RM3c" on this muster. May genuinely be RM (Radioman) rather than EM, but flag for verification.
