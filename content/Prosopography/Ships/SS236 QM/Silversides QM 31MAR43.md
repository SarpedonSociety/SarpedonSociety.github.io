## RM/EM Ambiguity Note

Three men show apparent cross-branch promotions from RM (Radioman) to CEM (Chief Electrician's Mate) between the two quarters: CALVER, CLARK (Arthur), and STEGALL. This is almost certainly a consistent E/R misread on the September 1942 muster. All three were likely EM ratings throughout, with their September rates being EM2c, EM1c, and EM1c respectively — making their March promotions (EM1c, CEM(AA), CEM(A)) standard progressions within the electrical branch.
