## The Last Man — LLOYD

**LLOYD, Calvin Stuart** [[658-03-20]]

```
19 Mar 1942  Received aboard USS Silversides. EM3c. Three months after commissioning.
             The plankowners are still all there. The war is three months old.

30 Sep 1942  EM3c. First quarterly muster.
31 Dec 1942  EM3c → EM2c. Promoted.
31 Mar 1943  EM2c. Still aboard.
30 Jun 1943  EM1c. Promoted again.
30 Sep 1943  EM1c.
31 Mar 1944  EM1c.
30 Jun 1944  EM1c. 27 months.
30 Sep 1944  EM1c. 30 months.
31 Dec 1944  CEM(AA)(T). MADE CHIEF. 33 months.
31 Mar 1945  CEM(AA)(T). PARSON leaves. LLOYD is now the longest-serving man. 36 months.
01 Jul 1945  CEM(AA)(T). 39 months. The war ends.
01 Oct 1945  CEM(AA)(T). 42 months. The final muster.
```

He didn't walk aboard on commissioning day. He arrived three months later. But he stayed — through fourteen war patrols, through the departure of every plankowner, through every quarterly muster from March 1942 to October 1945. Forty-two months. EM3c to Chief Electrician's Mate.

[[658-03-20]] is the thread that runs longest through the graph. Not from the first node, but from the second — and all the way to the last.
