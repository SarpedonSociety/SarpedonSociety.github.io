## [[295-54-23]] — End of the Line

**PARSON, Thomas Edward** is not on this muster.

After **36 months** — twelve quarterly musters, from December 15, 1941 to December 31, 1944 — the last man from commissioning day has left USS Silversides.

His final entry:
```
31 Dec 1944  CMoMM(AA)(T)  Chief Motor Machinist's Mate
             [[295-54-23]]  12/15/41
```

That was the last time 12/15/41 appeared in the received date column of a Silversides muster. It will not appear again.

**PSO NOTE** - He officially leaves on the ship on 17FEB45. Transferred to SubDiv-241 for duty. CMoMM(AA)(T).
