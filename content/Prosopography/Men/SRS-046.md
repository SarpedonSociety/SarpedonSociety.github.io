## Prewar (1904–1941)
Eusebio Zata was born in the Philippines on 22 July 1904. BIRLS gives his enlistment as 1 September 1928, and he was in San Diego in 1935. In the 1940 census he was thirty-six, enumerated at the Portsmouth Navy Yard (the entry first read Portsmouth, New Hampshire, and was corrected to Kittery, Maine) as a Mess Attendant aboard USS *Sailfish*.

## On *Silversides* (Dec 1941 – Jul 1942)
Zata commissioned aboard [[Silversides|*Silversides*]] on 15 December 1941 as OffCk2c and made her first war patrol under C.C. Burlingame. He left on 1 July 1942, two weeks before the second patrol sailed.

## Later War (1942–1945)
His other boats were USS *Fulton* and USS *Griffin*. The dates are not yet recorded.

## Postwar (1945–1963)
BIRLS gives his discharge as 1 September 1948. The 1950 census finds him in Vallejo, California, divorced, a roomer at 1250 Lewis in the home of Frances Crenshaw, a fifty-year-old widow; no occupation is listed. In 1955 he was nominated for sentry of the Manuel L. Quezon Post 603 of the American Legion. He died in September 1963 at the Travis Air Force Base hospital. His obituary in the *Times Herald* of 11 September called him a retired Navy cook who had lived in Vallejo for seventeen years and a member of the Quezon Post, the William Webb Post 893 of the Regular Veterans Association and Vallejo Branch 8 of the Fleet Reserve Association. He was survived by a cousin, Narciso Marpa of Richmond, and three sisters in the Philippines.
