## Prewar (1920–1941)
William Marsh was born on 27 February 1920. In 1940 he was twenty and living in Mosier, Oregon, in an owned $1,500 home with his parents, William A. (56), a carpenter, and Elsie (54). He worked as a farm laborer. He enlisted in the Navy on 11 June 1940.

## On *Silversides* (Dec 1941 – Feb 1944)
Marsh commissioned aboard [[Silversides|*Silversides*]] on 15 December 1941 as F3c. On his dates he made her first eight war patrols and left on 5 February 1944. Three other plankowners left the same day: [[SRS-004|Patrick A. Carswell]], [[SRS-005|Arthur W. Clark Jr.]] and [[SRS-035|Roy C. Riser]].

## Later War (1944–1945)
His service after leaving *Silversides* has not yet been traced.

## Postwar (1946–1983)
BIRLS gives his discharge as 10 June 1946 and a second period of service from 10 December 1951 to 4 January 1955. The 1950 census finds him in Columbus, Georgia, listed in the Armed Forces, with his wife Phyllis (19). On 17 December 1958 he married Dolorous Emily (Evans) Lewis; he was then a rancher and carpenter in Mosier, Oregon. He died on 13 March 1983.
