## Prewar (1917–1941)
Charles Mathewson was born on 20 November 1917 in Bingham, the son of Charles Edward and Agnes (Spray Paddock) Mathewson, according to his obituary. The 1920 census finds the family in Greenwood Township, Illinois, where his father was a farm laborer; Charles was two, with a sister, Ina (5). Before the war he served in *Sailfish* (SS-192), the former *Squalus*.

## On *Silversides* (Dec 1941 – Jul 1943)
Mathewson commissioned aboard [[Silversides|*Silversides*]] on 15 December 1941 as F1c. On 20 July 1943, the day before her sixth patrol sailed, he left for COMSUBDIV 82 for duty.

*Patrol table pending: his file counts four war patrols, but his dates bracket patrols 1 through 5.*

## Later War (1943–1945)
He was a plankowner of the new boat *Pilotfish* (SS-386), stayed aboard until 24 December 1944 and left as CMoMM(AA)(T). He was present for her first three war patrols.

## Postwar (1945–1996)
BIRLS records no World War II service and gives 20 November 1946 to 16 November 1950; a postwar compliance card supplied the birth date that matches it. He married Betty Fisher of New London, Connecticut, on 7 December 1945. The 1950 census finds him in New London, an Engineman in the Navy, with Betty (27) and their son Charles E. (3). He retired from the Navy after twenty-one years' service and worked for the W. M. Hales company of Hillsboro. He died on 6 January 1996, and his obituary appeared in the *Taylorville Daily Breeze-Courier* on 9 January.
