## Prewar (1921–1941)
Robert Danko was born in Youngstown, Ohio, on 10 September 1921, and later registered in Chicago. In 1930 the family owned a $3,200 home in Youngstown. His father Andrew (33) was a lineman for the telephone company, his mother Dora (27) kept house, and Robert (8) had a brother Wallace (4). By April 1940 his father had died. His widowed mother worked as a hotel housekeeper and rented a home at 3260 Harrison in Chicago for $30 a month, where Robert (19) drove a truck. He was in the Navy by May 1940. He served in *Sturgeon* (SS-187), the dates unrecorded.

## On *Silversides* (Dec 1941 – Jul 1943)
Danko commissioned aboard [[Silversides|*Silversides*]] on 15 December 1941 as S1c and made her first five war patrols, all under C.C. Burlingame, the last ending on 1 July 1943. On 15 July 1943, before the sixth patrol sailed, he transferred out. The *Honolulu Star-Bulletin* of 15 February 1943 lists him among those who took part in the emergency appendectomy performed during the patrol, together with [[SRS-042|Albert H. Stegall]].

## Later War (1943–1944)
He commissioned USS *Golet* (SS-361) on 30 November 1943 as QM1c and made both of her war patrols.

## Death (14 June 1944)
Danko was aboard *Golet* when she was lost on 14 June 1944. He was twenty-two. His status was recorded as missing on 12 July 1944 and as presumed dead on 18 January 1946.
