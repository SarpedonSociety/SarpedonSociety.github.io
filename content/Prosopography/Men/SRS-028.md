## Prewar (1916–1941)
Robert Messley was born on 3 October 1916. In 1930 the family lived in an owned $1,200 home in Springfield, Missouri. His father John (54) was a carpenter, and Robert, thirteen, was one of seven children. He enlisted in the Navy on 22 December 1936, at twenty.

## On *Silversides* (Dec 1941 – Dec 1944)
Messley commissioned aboard [[Silversides|*Silversides*]] on 15 December 1941 as F1c and made her first eleven war patrols, the first five under C.C. Burlingame and the rest under J.S. Coye Jr. He left on 8 December 1944 as CMoMM(AA), and [[SRS-033|Thomas E. Parson]] was advanced to Chief Motor Machinist the same day to replace him.

## Later War (1944–1945)
His service after leaving *Silversides* is not recorded in his file.

## Postwar (1945–1984)
BIRLS gives his discharge as 5 August 1947. The 1950 census finds him in North Campbell, Missouri, at 319 Homewood, with his wife Mildred (28) and daughter Donna Lynn (3); no occupation is listed. He died on 16 February 1984. His obituary in the *Springfield News-Leader* of 17 February called him a Navy veteran of World War II who served in the South Pacific and worked with the US Postal Service in New Haven.
