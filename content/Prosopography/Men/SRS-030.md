## Prewar (1914–1941)
Freeman Myers was born on 16 March 1914. In 1930 the family lived in Akron, Iowa, where his father James (41) and mother Esther (38) had Freeman (16), Phyllis (14), "Jenne" (12) and Ada (5). He enlisted in the Navy on 16 August 1933, and the *Sioux City Journal* announced it on 24 August. He served in *Salmon*; the dates are not yet recorded. The 1940 census finds him in San Diego, a Machinist's Mate Second Class who earned $844 in 1939, with his wife Ann (25) in a $25 rental at 930 Sampson Street.

## On *Silversides* (Dec 1941 – Mar 1942)
Myers commissioned aboard [[Silversides|*Silversides*]] on 15 December 1941 as MM1c. On 12 March 1942 he left for the Naval Hospital, San Diego, for treatment, which was before her first war patrol sailed. On 30 August 1942 he was back on the *Silversides* flag allowance (CSD 102) as CMM(AA), a date in the middle of her second patrol, so this was a paper attachment and not time aboard.

*No patrols aboard.*

## Later War (1942–1945)
From Submarine Division 102 he went to *Tunny* for a war patrol on 17 May 1943 as CMM(AA). He returned to the CSD 102 muster and was appointed Ensign by BuPers circular letter 122-43 on 9 August 1943; both changes are recorded on that day.

## Postwar (1945–1991)
BIRLS gives his discharge as 30 April 1955, and his Find a Grave memorial lists him as a Lieutenant in World War II and Korea. The 1950 census finds him at 3851 Bernice Drive in San Diego, in the Armed Forces, with his wife Neoma (28) and their two children, James (2) and Carol, born in October 1949; both children were born in Hawaii. He was no longer married to Ann. He died on 24 July 1991.
