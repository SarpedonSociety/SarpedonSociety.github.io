## Prewar (1917–1941)
Norris Duke was born on 9 April 1917. The 1930 census places the family in Orlando, Florida, in an owned home at 1408 Oregon Street valued at $1,500. His father John H. (37) was a mechanical dentist who owned his own dental laboratory, and Elma (28) worked there unpaid. Norris was thirteen, and his brother Charles was ten. He enlisted in the Navy on 17 April 1934, eight days after his seventeenth birthday.

## On *Silversides* (Dec 1941 – Feb 1942)
Duke commissioned aboard [[Silversides|*Silversides*]] on 15 December 1941 as SC1c. He left on 26 February 1942, about nine weeks before she sailed on her first war patrol, and made none.

## Later War (1942–1945)
From *Silversides* he went to the tender *Sperry*, then to LSV-1 and to shore billets. He left the submarine service entirely. The dates for these moves are not yet recorded.

## Postwar (1945–1986)
Duke stayed in the Navy until his BIRLS discharge on 21 November 1955, after more than twenty-one years. The 1950 census finds him in Anne Arundel County, Maryland, listed in the Armed Forces, with his wife Opal (28) and daughter Shirley (9). He died on 26 February 1986.
