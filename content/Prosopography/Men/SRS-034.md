## Prewar (1908–1941)
Richard Richardson Jr. was born on 24 August 1908. The 1920 census finds the family in Pheba, Mississippi, where his father Richard (40) was a doctor. His mother Gussie was 41, and the children were Charlton (11), Richard (12) and Cora (8). His original enlistment date is not known.

## On *Silversides* (Dec 1941 – Jul 1942)
Richardson commissioned aboard [[Silversides|*Silversides*]] on 15 December 1941 as PhM1c and made the boat's first war patrol under C.C. Burlingame. He left on 10 July 1942, five days before the second patrol sailed and on the same day as [[SRS-021|Elmer G. Huter]], and went to the Naval Hospital at Pearl Harbor for treatment.

## Later War (1942–1945)
From Pearl Harbor he went to San Francisco and then to Bremerton, where he joined the commissioning detail of USS *Carmick* on 8 October 1942. He disappears from the enlisted musters when he is appointed the physician of *Carmick*.

## Postwar (1945–1992)
He retired as a Commander in 1970, the year BIRLS gives for his discharge, and died on 2 October 1992.
