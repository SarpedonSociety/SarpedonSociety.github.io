## Prewar (1918–1941)
Darrell Robinson was born on 1 June 1918. In 1930 the family rented a farm at Spring Valley, Kansas. His father Roy (41) was the farmer, his mother Anne was 36, and Darrell (11) had a brother Lawrence (14). By 1940 he was a lodger in the household of Walter and Lilly Hynes at Spring Valley, working as a cook in a restaurant. His draft registration was with Tulsa County Board No. 2, and describes a man 5 ft 8 in and 150 lb with light complexion, blue eyes and brown hair.

## On *Silversides* (Dec 1941 – )
Robinson commissioned aboard [[Silversides|*Silversides*]] on 15 December 1941 as Sea2c. The enlisted records do not yet show when he left or how many patrols he made.

## Later War (1944–1945)
He was a plankowner of USS *Cobia* (SS-245), the Gato-class boat now preserved as a museum ship at Manitowoc, Wisconsin, when she commissioned on 29 March 1944 as SC1c.

## Postwar (1945–2005)
BIRLS gives a Navy period from 5 November 1946 to 29 October 1951. The 1950 census finds him in Key West, Florida, in the Armed Forces, with his wife Reba (26) and two young children. He reached Chief Commissary Steward and died on 30 November 2005.
