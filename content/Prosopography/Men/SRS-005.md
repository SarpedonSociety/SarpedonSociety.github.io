## Prewar (1918–1941)
Arthur Wilson Clark Jr. was born on 17 November 1918, five months after the death of his father. Arthur Sr., born 20 October 1894, was a signalman in a logging camp and was killed in June 1918 when a falling tree fractured his skull.

The 1920 census finds the thirteen-month-old, enumerated as "Junior A. Clark," in Los Angeles in the household of his maternal grandmother, Alice Randall. The house held several Randall and Oliver relatives and four lodgers. He joined the Navy before the war and was a plankowner of the new fleet boat *Sculpin*; his enlistment date has not yet been established.

## On *Silversides* (Dec 1941 – Feb 1944)
Clark commissioned aboard [[Silversides|*Silversides*]] on 15 December 1941 as EM2c and stayed for her first eight war patrols, the first five under C.C. Burlingame and the next three under J.S. Coye Jr. He rose to Chief Electrician's Mate and left the boat on 5 February 1944, after the eighth patrol and ten days before the ninth sailed. Three other plankowners left on the same date: [[SRS-004|Patrick A. Carswell]], [[SRS-026|William F. Marsh]] and [[SRS-035|Roy C. Riser]]. His file does not record where he went.
