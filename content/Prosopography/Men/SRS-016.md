## Prewar (1910–1941)
Donald Finch was born on 27 September 1910. In 1930 the family owned a multifamily home at 805 Puget in Sedro-Woolley, Washington, valued at $2,500, with two families renting. The house had a radio. His father Oscar (46) was a railroad agent, and Donald (19) worked as a laborer. His brother Douglass (23) was an orchestra musician, and the household included Raymond (17), Genieve (14) and his mother Nina (47). He enlisted in the Navy on 1 August 1931, at twenty. In August 1941 he left for the *Snapper* flag allowance of CSD 22 on the same date as the *Sailfish* group.

## On *Silversides* (Dec 1941 – Sep 1942)
Finch commissioned aboard [[Silversides|*Silversides*]] on 15 December 1941 as EM1c. He was a CEM(AA) by September 1942, and on 17 September he was appointed Ensign (T) on orders dated 15 August 1942, the same day [[SRS-029|Alvin R. Mumme]] was appointed Electrician (T). He then leaves the enlisted records as a mustang, and it is unclear how many patrols he made with the boat.
