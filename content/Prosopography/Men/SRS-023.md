## Prewar (c. 1906–1941)
Naturalization papers name Peter Lewis as a child of John Lewis, formerly Jan Janelunas, of Puipai, Lithuania, who emigrated from Hamburg aboard the SS *Belgravia* on 14 November 1899. The 1940 census finds Peter, 34, in San Diego with his wife Anna (37) in a $25 rental, listed as a machinist's mate in the Navy. He had earlier served in *Sculpin*.

## On *Silversides* (Dec 1941 – Dec 1942)
Lewis commissioned aboard [[Silversides|*Silversides*]] on 15 December 1941 as MM1c. On his dates he made her first three war patrols under C.C. Burlingame. He left on 11 December 1942, six days before the fourth patrol sailed.

| Patrol | Dates | CO | Credited sunk | Credited damaged |

## Later War (1943–1945)
His service after leaving *Silversides* has not yet been traced.

## Postwar (1945–)
The 1950 census finds him and Anna (48) in Connecticut, where he worked as a wire coverer at a wire manufacturing plant. His death is not yet recorded.
