## Prewar (1915–1941)
Andrew Smiley was born in Rome, New York, on 10 November 1915. In 1930 the family owned a $2,000 home with a radio in Lysander, New York. His father Andrew W. (40) was a general laborer, and his mother Rachel (38) was born Rachel Maurer, which gives him his middle name. Andrew (14) had siblings Kathryn (10), Raymond (8), Florence (6), James (3) and John (8 months). Raymond also served in the Navy in World War II. Andrew enlisted on 20 April 1933, aged seventeen. The 1940 census finds him at the Submarine Base, Pearl Harbor, as A. Maurer Smiley, 24, a Torpedoman Second Class.

## On *Silversides* (Dec 1941 – Sep 1943)
Smiley commissioned aboard [[Silversides|*Silversides*]] on 15 December 1941 as TM1c and made her first six war patrols, the first five under C.C. Burlingame and the sixth under J.S. Coye Jr. He was a chief, recorded as CTMM(P), when he accepted a commission as Ensign on 26 September 1943, on an authorization dated 15 August, after the sixth patrol that credited nothing. He disappears from the enlisted rolls that day.

## Later War (1943–1945)
He was promoted Lieutenant (junior grade) on 1 December 1944. The 1945 Navy register shows (E) and (G-1) after his name.

## Postwar (1945–1999)
BIRLS gives his discharge as 16 April 1947, which ends the enlisted portion of his record. The Navy registers show him as a Lieutenant by 1 January 1949, and he reached Commander in January 1956 and retired that month. He died on 31 January 1999 in Kitsap County, Washington.
