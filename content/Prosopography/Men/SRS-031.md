## Prewar (1919–1941)
William Newton was born in Philadelphia on 30 December 1919 and enlisted on his nineteenth birthday, 30 December 1938. His father James, a locomotive engineer, died on 19 September 1930. His brother John Robert, the youngest, also served in the Navy, enlisting in Philadelphia on 25 September 1942. After the war William moved to Rome, Georgia, so he is easily confused with a Georgia-born William Henry Newton, a seaman aboard USS *Whitney* in the 1930 census, who is a different man.

## On *Silversides* (Dec 1941 – Dec 1942)
Newton commissioned aboard [[Silversides|*Silversides*]] on 15 December 1941 as TM2c and made her first three war patrols under C.C. Burlingame. He left on 11 December 1942, six days before the fourth patrol sailed, for ComSubDiv 102 "for duty."

## Later War (1943–1945)
He was a plankowner of two more Gato-class boats. USS *Bonefish* (SS-223) commissioned on 31 May 1943, and he came aboard as TM1c. On 16 July he was sent to the Submarine Base, New London, for treatment, and his time in her was cut short by a naval hospital stay. He rejoined the building crew of USS *Flasher* (SS-249), which commissioned on 25 September 1943, and stayed through her fifth war patrol. He transferred on 3 January 1945 to Submarine Division 182, flag allowance and relief crew, and BIRLS gives his discharge as 9 November 1945.

## Postwar (1945–1986)
Newton moved to Georgia and became an executive of the Primrose Tapestry Company, the textile firm's southern branch, rising to vice president. He married Susie Pauline Branch in 1950. He died on 3 August 1986.
