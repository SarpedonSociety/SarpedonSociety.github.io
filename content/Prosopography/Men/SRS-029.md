## Prewar (1902–1941)
Alvin Mumme was born on 5 August 1902 in Texas. In 1910 the family lived in Bexar County, where his father Charles (43) was a farmer. Alvin (7) had brothers Hugo (20) and Eddie (15) and sisters Lillie (10), Rubie (5) and Evelyn (2). He enlisted in the Navy on 9 October 1925, at twenty-three. His residence in April 1935 was Honolulu. The 1940 census finds him in San Diego, an electrician in the Navy who earned $2,100 in 1939, with his wife Mary (35) and a nephew, Henry Rush (13), in a $26 rental.

## On *Silversides* (Dec 1941 – Sep 1942)
Mumme commissioned aboard [[Silversides|*Silversides*]] on 15 December 1941 as CEM (PA) and made her first two war patrols under C.C. Burlingame. He entered the wardroom after the second patrol, appointed an Electrician (T) with the officer number 204065, and disappears from the enlisted ROCs and musters. The ROC date is 17 September 1942, while the Military Register records 15 August 1942.

## Later War (1943–1945)
He was detached from USS *Medusa* on 27 March 1944 as a Chief Electrician, listed with two lieutenants (junior grade) and a lieutenant commander and without a destination, which puts him in a repair ship. He later reached CWO2.

## Postwar (1945–1988)
The 1950 census finds him in San Diego, an electrician who called himself a private worker, with his wife Mary. He reported $3,400 in wages for 29 weeks in 1949 and another $2,700 as veterans' pension or other income, and he checked World War II veteran. He retired as a Chief Warrant Officer after thirty years in the Navy and died at home in Allied Gardens on 29 May 1988. His obituary in the San Diego *Evening Tribune* of 2 June says he was a native of Texas and a resident of the county for sixty-eight years, with services at Greenwood Mortuary and entombment in Greenwood Mausoleum. His wife Gwendolyn, three daughters and two sisters survived him.
