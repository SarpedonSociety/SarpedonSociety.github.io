## Prewar (1918–1941)
James Watson was born in Wilson, North Carolina, on 10 August 1918. His postwar compliance card names his uncle, Willie Currie, as next of kin and gives his uncle's address, 304 East Walnut Street in Wilson, as the address it was filed from. No census record for him before 1950 has been found. He enlisted in the Navy on 17 November 1939, at twenty-one.

## On *Silversides* (Dec 1941 – Apr 1942)
Watson commissioned aboard [[Silversides|*Silversides*]] on 15 December 1941 as a Mess Attendant Second Class. In mid-April 1942 he left for the submarine tender *Fulton*, less than two weeks before *Silversides* sailed on her first war patrol, and made none.

## Later War (1942–1945)
His wartime service after *Fulton* has not yet been traced. BIRLS gives his discharge as 22 December 1945.

## Postwar (1945–1988)
The 1950 census finds him in Los Angeles, working as a street cleaner for the city's Public Works department, with his wife Beatrice (30). No children were in the household. He died on 6 August 1988, and BIRLS confirms the date.
