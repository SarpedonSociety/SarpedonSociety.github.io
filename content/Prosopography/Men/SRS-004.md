## Prewar (1923–1941)
Patrick Carswell was born on 18 September 1923. The 1940 census finds him at sixteen in Charleston, South Carolina, in a $33 rental with his widowed father Ray, a stenographer at the Navy Yard who had earned $1,740 in 1939, and Michael (17). He enlisted in the Navy on 30 September 1940, at seventeen.

## On *Silversides* (Dec 1941 – Feb 1944)
Carswell commissioned aboard [[Silversides|*Silversides*]] on 15 December 1941 as SM3c, aged eighteen. He made her first eight war patrols and left on 5 February 1944 as QM1c. Three other plankowners left the same day: [[SRS-005|Arthur W. Clark Jr.]], [[SRS-026|William F. Marsh]] and [[SRS-035|Roy C. Riser]].

## Later War (1944–1945)
His service after leaving *Silversides* has not yet been traced.

## Postwar (1945–2013)
BIRLS gives his discharge as 19 October 1945. The 1950 census finds him in Charleston working as a telephone installation and repairman for the telephone company, with his wife Mary (22). A Korean War-era draft card gives his final rate as Chief Quartermaster. He died on 11 May 2013.
