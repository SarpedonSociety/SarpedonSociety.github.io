## Prewar (1921–1941)
Howard Calver was born in Lowville, New York, on 28 February 1921, the son of Lyle A. and Elsie Calver. In 1930 the family rented a home in Lowville village for $20 a month. Lyle was a printer at the newspaper, and the household included Howard's older brother Theodore, his sister Berdina and his younger brother Donald. By the 1940 census the family owned a $1,400 home. Lyle had earned $1,664 in 1939, Theodore had three years of college, and Howard, 19, a high-school graduate, was listed as a new worker.

The musters place him in *Sturgeon* (SS-187) from 1940 until 21 August 1941, when he transferred to the flag allowance of CSD 22 aboard *Snapper*.

## On *Silversides* (Dec 1941 – Nov 1943)
Calver commissioned aboard [[Silversides|*Silversides*]] on 15 December 1941 as EM3c and made her first seven war patrols, the first five under C.C. Burlingame and the next two under J.S. Coye Jr. On 23 November 1943 he left for COMSUBDIV 102 for duty.

## Later War (1943–1945)
He was a plankowner of the new boat *Besugo* (SS-321) and held the rate of EM1c at her commissioning. BIRLS leaves out his enlisted service and records only 30 November 1944 to 10 June 1960.

## Postwar (1945–2010)
The 1950 census finds him in New London, Connecticut, listed in the Armed Forces, with his wife Reba (28), their children William Howard (5) and Carol Ann (2), and an infant son, Andrew. BIRLS gives his discharge as 10 June 1960. He died on 8 May 2010, aged 89, at the Bridges of Bent Creek in Mechanicsburg. His obituary names him a member of St. Joseph Catholic Church, the Knights of Columbus, American Legion Post 109, Catholic War Veterans Post 1738, West Shore Elks Lodge 2257, the Fleet Reserve Association and the Sub Vets of WWII. He was the widower of Reba and was preceded in death by his brother Theodore.
