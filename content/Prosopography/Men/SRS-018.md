## Prewar (1913–1941)
Albert Gutzmer was born on 3 June 1913. In 1930 his widowed father Otto (57), who was born in Germany, farmed at Silver Creek, South Dakota, with seven children at home, among them Albert (16). Albert enlisted in the Navy on 22 July 1930, three months after the census was taken.

## On *Silversides* (Dec 1941 – Jul 1943)
Gutzmer commissioned aboard [[Silversides|*Silversides*]] on 15 December 1941 as EM1c. He left on 28 March 1943 for U.S. Naval Mobile Hospital No. 2, returned on 8 May 1943, and made the fifth patrol, so he was aboard for the first five. His final exit was on 15 July 1943, to ComSubDiv 82 "for duty," as a CEM(AA).

## Later War (1943–1945)
Orders to USS *Bream* (SS-243) were cut on 7 September 1943. He was a plankowner when she commissioned on 24 January 1944 as CEM(PA), made one war patrol and left on 14 July 1944 for the CSD 181 relief crew. He was commissioned Ensign on 15 February 1945. *Skipjack* (SS-184) also appears among his boats, dates unrecorded.

## Postwar (1945–1995)
The 1950 census finds him in Philadelphia, listed in the Armed Forces, with his wife Lorita (37) and one child. BIRLS gives his discharge as 1 October 1957, and his Find a Grave memorial lists him as a Lieutenant in World War II and Korea. He died on 20 November 1995.
