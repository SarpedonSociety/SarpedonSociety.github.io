## Prewar (1918–1941)
James Duckworth was born on 4 April 1918, and his compliance card gives Fort Wood, West Virginia. In 1930 the family rented a house for $5 a month in Black Fork, Tucker County. His father Ford (36) was a teamster in the logging yards, and his mother Maud (36) kept a household of eleven children, of whom James (12) was the fourth. He enlisted in the Navy on 9 December 1936, at eighteen. He served in *Sailfish* (SS-192), and on 21 August 1941 he went to the *Snapper* flag allowance of CSD 22, the same date and change as [[SRS-016|Donald E. Finch]] and [[SRS-010|Lester J. De Muth]].

## On *Silversides* (Dec 1941 – Sep 1943)
Duckworth commissioned aboard [[Silversides|*Silversides*]] on 15 December 1941 as TM3c. He stayed for her first six war patrols, from 30 April 1942 to 12 September 1943, and was transferred on 27 September 1943 to ComSubDiv 82 "for duty" instead of for new construction.

## Later War (1943–1945)
His service has a gap between 27 September 1943 and 4 July 1944, when he commissioned USS *Blackfin* (SS-322). He made her first war patrol and was detached to CSD 182 at Fremantle on 5 December 1944. That unit advanced him to CTM(AA)(T) on 1 February 1945 and sent him to the Submarine Base, New London, on 6 February as an instructor. He reported to New London on 23 May 1945, and on 2 August he went to Naval Hospital Newport and on 17 August to Naval Hospital Sampson.

## Postwar (1945–1980)
BIRLS gives his separation as 20 December 1946, and on 24 March 1947 he completed a postwar compliance card from Poquonnock Bridge, Connecticut, listing his employer as "Veteran-Disch-Navy." The card describes a man 5 ft 9 in and 150 lb, with light complexion, brown hair and eyes, tattoos on his right leg and left arm, and a birthmark on his left arm. The 1950 census finds him in Coketon, West Virginia, "unable to work," with his wife Melda (28) and three children, the youngest born in Connecticut. He died on 19 August 1980.
