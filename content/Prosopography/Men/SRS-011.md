## Prewar (1920–1941)
Marvin Ducharme was born in Red Lake Falls, Minnesota, on 1 October 1920. In 1930 the family rented a house for $12 a month. His father Albert (37) was an unskilled laborer, and Marvin (9) was one of seven children then at home. By 1940 the household had grown to eleven children, with Albert working as a maintenance man for the State Highway Department. Marvin (19) was a laborer at the creamery, while his brothers Fay and Donald worked at the pulp mill and a dry cleaner. Three of his brothers served in the war: Paul as a Captain in the Army Air Forces, Fay in the South Pacific, and Donald as an Army Captain in World War II and Korea. His enlistment date is not yet established.

## On *Silversides* (Dec 1941 – Feb 1942)
Ducharme commissioned aboard [[Silversides|*Silversides*]] on 15 December 1941 as Y3c, one of the boat's yeomen. He left on 21 February 1942, about ten weeks before she sailed on her first war patrol, and made none.

*No patrols aboard.*

## Later War (1942–1944)
His file lists *Sargo* (SS-188) and the tender *Sperry* among his boats, without dates, and he reached the rate of Chief Yeoman (Acting Appointment). By 1944 he was in the Southwest Pacific.

## Death (18 June 1944)
Ducharme was listed missing on 18 June 1944 from Motor Torpedo Boat Base No. 4, "In the China Straits, New Guinea." He was twenty-three. No craft is named, and the nature of the loss is not yet known. His place of burial or memorial is not recorded in the file.
