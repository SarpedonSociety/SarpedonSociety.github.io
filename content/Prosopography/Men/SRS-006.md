## Prewar (1920–1941)
Robert Cramer was born in Springfield, Illinois, on 21 May 1920. Nothing is yet recorded of his family or boyhood. He enlisted in the Navy on 11 July 1938, at eighteen.

## On *Silversides* (Dec 1941 – Apr 1942)
Cramer commissioned aboard [[Silversides|*Silversides*]] on 15 December 1941 as EM3c. The muster remarks record that on 23 March 1942 he left his station before being properly relieved, and that a deck court on 25 March reduced him to Seaman First Class. On 2 April he left the boat for the U.S. Naval Hospital at Mare Island for treatment. *Silversides* sailed on her first war patrol on 30 April, so he made none.

## Later War (1942–1944)
He made one war patrol in *Flying Fish* (SS-229) and was then sent to *Sperry*. *Stingray* (SS-186) also appears among his boats. The dates for all three are not yet recorded.

## Postwar (1944–2000)
BIRLS gives his discharge as 12 October 1944. The 1950 census finds him in Fullerton, California, working as an embalmer at a mortuary, with his wife Maxine (28). He died on 3 November 2000.
