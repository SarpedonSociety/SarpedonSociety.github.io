## Prewar (1918–1941)
Roy Riser was born in Steubenville, Ohio, on 14 November 1918. In 1930 he was eleven and living at 330 Lake Erie in a $30 rental with his grandparents, John M. Bufford (75) and Ella (72). He was the only child in the house. The two boarders, Mike Steve (29) and Lewis Anastas (30), were steel-mill laborers, and nobody else in the household worked. He enlisted in the Navy on 21 November 1938, a week after his twentieth birthday.

## On *Silversides* (Dec 1941 – Feb 1944)
Riser commissioned aboard [[Silversides|*Silversides*]] on 15 December 1941 as MM2c and made her first eight war patrols. He left on 5 February 1944. Three other plankowners left the same day: [[SRS-004|Patrick A. Carswell]], [[SRS-005|Arthur W. Clark Jr.]] and [[SRS-026|William F. Marsh]].

## Later War (1944–1945)
He joined the new boat *Trutta* (SS-421) five days after her commissioning and stayed aboard through the end of the war. He reached CWO2 during the war and retired as CWO3.

## Postwar (1945–1994)
The 1950 census finds him in Key West, Florida, listed in the Armed Forces, with his wife Carrie (29) and their son Richard (5). He moved to Sarasota from Canton, Ohio, about 1967 and died there on 3 February 1994, aged 75. His obituary in the *Sarasota Herald-Tribune* says he received a Presidential Unit Citation and the Silver and Bronze Stars, and belonged to the US Submarine Veterans of World War II and the Retired Officers' Club in Sarasota. He was a Methodist and was survived by his wife, a son, three sisters, two brothers and two grandchildren.
