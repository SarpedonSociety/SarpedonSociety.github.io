## Prewar (1916–1941)
August Ballman was born on 13 November 1916. In 1930 the family rented a home in Union, New Jersey, for $42 a month and owned a radio. His father Charles (38) was a druggist, and the household included his mother Theresa (33) and his brothers Charles (11), Harry (7) and Theodore (1). His service number falls in the 223- block assigned to the Third Naval District (New York), which fits Union rather than San Diego. By 1940 he was twenty-three and a Torpedoman First Class in the submarine service, living in San Diego with his wife Eileen (20) in a $23 rental.

## On *Silversides* (Dec 1941 – )
Ballman commissioned aboard [[Silversides|*Silversides*]] on 15 December 1941 as TM1c. The enlisted records do not show when he left or how many patrols he made. He was later commissioned an Ensign with the officer service number O-201779, and his enlisted number closes at the commission.

## Later War (1942–1943)
His brother Charles Joseph Ballman (service no. 223-56-73, MM2c) served in USS *Wahoo* and was detached to CSD 44 on 19 April 1943, the day Olin Jacobs joined her. Whether August was a plankowner of *Dorado* can be confirmed only from his OMPF.

## Death (12 Oct 1943)
Ballman was lost with USS *Dorado* (SS-248) on 12 October 1943, a day after *Wahoo* was lost with Olin Jacobs. Charles, having left *Wahoo* in April, survived her loss as far as is known.
