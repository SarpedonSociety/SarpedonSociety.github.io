## Prewar (1905–1941)
Theodore Duncan was born in Winfield, Tennessee, on 22 February 1905, the son of William Duncan and Blanie Tramell, both of Winfield. He was thirty-six at *Silversides*'s commissioning and already a Chief. His enlistment date and earlier service have not yet been established.

## On *Silversides* (Dec 1941 – May 1943)
Duncan commissioned aboard [[Silversides|*Silversides*]] on 15 December 1941 as a Chief Machinist's Mate with a permanent appointment. He made her first four war patrols under C.C. Burlingame. On 8 May 1943 he left to serve as an instructor at the Submarine Base, New London.

## Later War (1943–1945)
Nothing is yet recorded of his service after the instructor assignment.

## Postwar (1945–1994)
The 1950 census finds him in San Francisco with his wife Bernice (44), listed as Retired USN and unable to work. He died on 6 December 1994.
