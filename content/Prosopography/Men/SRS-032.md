## Prewar (1915–1941)
Delmont Pace was born on 26 June 1915. In 1930 the family rented a house in Birmingham, Alabama, for $23 a month and owned a radio. His father A.T. (42) was a salesman at a roofing company, his mother Mollie was 42, and Delmont (14) had a sister Louise (20), who kept books for a real estate firm, a brother Paul (16), who sold hamburgers, and three younger sisters. His enlistment date is not yet established.

## On *Silversides* (Dec 1941 – 1942)
Pace commissioned aboard [[Silversides|*Silversides*]] on 15 December 1941 as TM1c. He was gone before the second patrol sailed on 15 July 1942, since he joined the building crew of USS *Runner* (SS-275), which commissioned on 30 July 1942. Whether he made the first patrol (30 April – 21 June) is not yet established.

## Later War (1942–1943)
He was a plankowner of *Runner*, a Gato-class boat, as TM1c and rose to Chief Torpedoman's Mate (Acting). He was aboard when she was lost in 1943.

## Death (1943)
*Runner* was lost with her crew, and the file dates Pace's loss to 11 July 1943. His status was changed to presumed dead on 2 January 1946, and a page of *The Birmingham News* for 13 January 1946 is in the file. He was twenty-eight.
