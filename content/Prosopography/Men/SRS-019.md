## Prewar (1918–1941)
Mike Harbin was born on 30 June 1918. In the 1930 census he is the fifth of seven children in the McClain County, Oklahoma, household of William and Eudie Harbin. William farmed rented land, and the farm had a radio. The eldest child, Ethel (20), taught at the public school. The others were Johnnie (17), Pat (15), Kelly (13), Mike (11), Martha (10) and Jerry (9). His enlistment date and early service have not yet been established.

## On *Silversides* (Dec 1941 – May 1942)
Harbin commissioned aboard [[Silversides|*Silversides*]] on 15 December 1941 as a Seaman First Class and held the rate of Torpedoman's Mate Third Class by the time of his death. He sailed on her first war patrol, which left Pearl Harbor on 30 April 1942.

¹ Tonnage as credited in endorsements. Patrol 1 includes a submarine credit that was likely a log or other debris. Harbin did not complete the patrol; the figures are the patrol's totals.

## Death and Burial (10 May 1942)
On 10 May 1942, ten days out of Pearl Harbor, Harbin was killed in a surface gun action with an enemy trawler, at 33°13′30″ N, 151°57′30″ E. He was twenty-three. His remains were buried at sea with appropriate ceremonies at 1900 that day, at the same position.
