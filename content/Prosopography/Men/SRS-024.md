## Prewar (1921–1941)
Ernest Lucas was born on 15 November 1921. In 1930 the family lived in Canaan Township, Ohio, where his father Perry (32) was a farmer. Ernest was one of five brothers: Cecil, Clifford, Richard and Howard, the youngest at five months. His enlistment date has not yet been established.

## On *Silversides* (Dec 1941 – Sep 1942)
Lucas commissioned aboard [[Silversides|*Silversides*]] on 15 December 1941 as Seaman First Class and was rated Ship's Cook Third Class when he left on 25 September 1942. On his dates he made her first two war patrols under C.C. Burlingame.

| Patrol | Dates | CO | Credited sunk | Credited damaged |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | 30 Apr – 21 Jun 1942 | Burlingame | 5 (25,627 t)¹ | 1 (10,100 t) |
| 2 | 15 Jul – 8 Sep 1942 | Burlingame | 4 (15,250 t) | 2 (5,750 t) |
| **Total** | | | **9 (40,877 t)** | **3 (15,850 t)** |

¹ Tonnage as credited in endorsements. Patrol 1 includes a submarine credit that was likely a log or other debris.

## Later War (1942–1945)
His wartime service after *Silversides* has not yet been traced.

## Postwar (1945–2013)
The 1950 census enumerates him aboard USS *Chivo* (SS-341), a Balao-class submarine, at the Submarine Base, Key West. He retired from the Navy in 1961 after twenty-two years' service in World War II and Korea, and his headstone reads "CPO – US Navy" beside a submarine and anchor. His obituary says he retired in 1985 from the Chillicothe VA Medical Center, where he was Director of Social Services and Utilization Review, and had also retired from Adena Regional Medical Center after fifteen years in the same post. He belonged to the National Association of Social Workers and the Ohio Hospital Association of Social Workers. He died on 11 December 2013.
