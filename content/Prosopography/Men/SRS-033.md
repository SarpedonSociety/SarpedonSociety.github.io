## Prewar (1917–1941)
Thomas Parson was born on 31 August 1917. In 1930 the family lived in Tracy City, Tennessee, in an owned home valued at $200. His father Thomas J. (46) was a coal miner, and his sister Lillie (21) was a public school teacher. Thomas, twelve, was one of five children. Before the war he served in *Sailfish* (SS-192) and appears on the same muster as [[SRS-027|Charles H. Mathewson]].

## On *Silversides* (Dec 1941 – Feb 1945)
Parson commissioned aboard [[Silversides|*Silversides*]] on 15 December 1941 as F2c and made her first twelve war patrols, the first five under C.C. Burlingame, the next six under J.S. Coye Jr. and the twelfth under J.C. Nichols. On 8 December 1944 he was advanced to Chief Motor Machinist to replace [[SRS-028|Robert F. Messley]]. He left on 17 February 1945 as CMoMM(AA)(T).

## Later War (1945)
His service after leaving *Silversides* has not yet been traced.

## Postwar (1945–1990)
BIRLS omits his World War II service and records 2 February 1956 to 31 July 1962. A 1955 Chattanooga city directory lists him as a chief petty officer in charge of the US Navy Recruiting Station, married to Margaret. No 1950 census entry has been found. He died on 14 October 1990.
